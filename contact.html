Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our services

How long does it take to get a shipping quote?

We typically provide shipping quotes within 2-4 business hours during regular business days. For urgent requests, you can call us directly at +91 8433667514 for immediate assistance.

What areas do you serve?

We provide cargo services across all major cities in India including Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, and Pune. We also offer international shipping services to select countries.

How can I track my shipment?

You can track your shipment using the tracking number provided at the time of booking. Visit our tracking page or call our customer service for real-time updates on your shipment status.

What documents are required for shipping?

Required documents vary based on the type of shipment. Generally, you'll need a government-issued ID, address proof, and details of the items being shipped. Our team will guide you through the specific requirements.

Do you offer insurance for shipments?

Yes, we offer comprehensive insurance options for all types of shipments. Insurance coverage can be customized based on the value and nature of your goods. Contact our team for detailed information about insurance plans.

Need Immediate Assistance?

Our customer support team is available 24/7 to help with urgent shipping needs, tracking inquiries, and emergency situations.
